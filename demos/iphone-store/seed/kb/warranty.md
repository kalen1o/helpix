# Limited warranty

Every Orchard phone and Orchard Buds purchase includes a limited warranty. It protects you against problems that come from how the product was made.

## Coverage period

The warranty lasts one year from the date of purchase. Keep your order confirmation email, because we use it to confirm that date.

## What is covered

The warranty covers manufacturing defects in the hardware, such as a battery that fails early, a faulty speaker, or a screen that develops dead pixels without any impact. If we confirm a defect, we repair the product or replace it at no cost to you.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover accidental damage, such as drops and cracked screens. It does not cover liquid damage. It also does not cover repairs or modifications carried out by anyone other than Orchard Store or our authorised partners. Normal wear, such as scratches on the casing, is not a defect.

## Making a claim

To make a claim, email support@orchard.demo with your order number, a short description of the problem and the serial number of the product. The serial number is in Settings under About, or printed on the box. We reply within one business day with the next steps.

If you want cover for accidents as well, see the Orchard Care protection plan.
