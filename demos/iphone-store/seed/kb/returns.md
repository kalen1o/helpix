# Returns and refunds

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If you are not, you can send it back within 30 days of delivery, for any reason.

## What can be returned

To qualify, the device must be in its original condition and returned with all accessories that came in the box, including cables, documentation and packaging where possible. Devices with signs of heavy wear or damage may be refused or refunded at a reduced amount.

## Earbuds

For hygiene reasons, opened Orchard Buds can be returned only if they are defective. Unopened earbuds can be returned within the same 30 days as any other product.

## How to return

Start a return by emailing support@orchard.demo with your order number. We send you a free prepaid return label, so you never pay for return shipping. Pack the item securely, attach the label and drop it off with the carrier.

## Refunds

Once your return arrives, we inspect it. We then refund the original payment method within 5 business days of inspection. Your bank may need a few additional days to show the money in your account.

Refunds cover the item price and any shipping you paid. Orchard Care protection plan purchases are refunded together with the device.
