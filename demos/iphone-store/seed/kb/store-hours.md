# Support hours and contact

Orchard Store is an online shop, so there is no counter to walk up to. Here is how to reach us and when to expect an answer.

## Chat assistant

Our chat assistant is available 24/7. It can answer questions about shipping, returns, the warranty, the Orchard Care protection plan and trade-ins at any hour, including weekends and holidays.

## Email support

For anything the assistant cannot resolve, write to support@orchard.demo. A member of our team replies within one business day. Include your order number so we can help faster.

## Phone support

You can call us Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm ET, on (555) 010-0199. Calls outside those hours go to voicemail, and we return them the next business day.

## Physical stores

Orchard Store has no physical stores. Everything, including returns, repairs and trade-ins, is handled online or by mail with prepaid labels, so you never have to travel anywhere.

## Order questions

For questions about a specific order, email support with your order number and the name used at checkout.
