# Trade-in program

Trade in your old phone and put the value toward a new one. It is an easy way to save money and keep a working device out of a drawer.

## What you can trade in

You can trade in any Orchard phone, or a phone from another brand, as long as it is in working condition. It should turn on, hold a charge and have a screen that responds to touch. Phones that are locked to an account or reported lost or stolen cannot be accepted.

## How much you get

Trade-in credit ranges from $50 to $650. The exact amount depends on the model and its condition. Newer models in excellent condition receive the most, while older or heavily worn phones receive less. You see an estimate before you commit, and the final value is confirmed after inspection.

## How it works

1. Tell us which phone you have and describe its condition.
2. We send you a prepaid trade-in kit with a box and a shipping label.
3. Erase your data, pack the phone and send it back.
4. We inspect it and apply your credit within 7 days of inspection.

The credit is applied to your Orchard Store account and can be used on any phone or accessory. Questions about a trade-in can go to support@orchard.demo.
