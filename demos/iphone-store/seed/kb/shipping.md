# Shipping and delivery

Orchard Store ships every order from our fulfilment centre in the contiguous United States. This page explains what delivery options you have and how long they take.

## Standard shipping

Shipping is free and takes two days on every order delivered in the contiguous US. There is no minimum spend and no code to enter. The delivery estimate is shown at checkout before you pay.

## Next-day delivery

If you need your order faster, choose next-day delivery for $15. Place your order by 2 pm ET and it ships the same day, arriving the following day. Orders placed after 2 pm ET ship the next business day.

## Alaska and Hawaii

Orders to Alaska and Hawaii take 3 to 5 days. Free shipping applies to these destinations as well, but next-day delivery is not available there.

## International orders

We do not ship internationally. Orchard Store delivers only within the United States.

## Tracking your order

As soon as your order ships, we send a tracking email with a link to follow the parcel. If you have not received a tracking email after one business day, write to support@orchard.demo with your order number and we will look into it.
