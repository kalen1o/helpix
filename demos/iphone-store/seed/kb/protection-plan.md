# Orchard Care protection plan

Orchard Care is an optional plan that extends the standard warranty to cover accidents. It is available for Orchard phones and Orchard Buds.

## Pricing

Choose either $9.99 per month, which you can cancel at any time, or $199 for two years, paid up front. The two-year option costs less than paying monthly for the same period.

## What you get

Orchard Care includes everything in the limited warranty, and adds accidental-damage cover. You can make two incidents per year. Each incident carries a fixed service fee:

- A cracked or broken screen costs $29 to repair.
- Any other accidental damage, including liquid damage, costs $99.

Repairs are done with genuine Orchard parts. If a device cannot be repaired, we replace it with an equivalent model.

## When to buy

You must add Orchard Care within 60 days of purchasing your device. After 60 days the plan can no longer be added, so decide early. You can add it at checkout or by emailing support@orchard.demo with your order number.

## Making a claim

Email support@orchard.demo with the serial number of the device and a short description of what happened. We send a prepaid shipping kit and keep you updated while the repair is in progress.
